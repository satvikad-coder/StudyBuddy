Who we are

A free, no-pressure way to get exam-ready — built by people who remember how loud test anxiety can get.

We started StudyPrep Resources because good SHSAT and SAT prep shouldn't sit behind a paywall. Every core resource on this site — practice questions, guides, pacing strategies — is free. Where a paid resource is genuinely worth the money, we'll tell you that too. As you keep scrolling, we'll show you a few things worth knowing before test day.

Worth knowing

Things students find out the hard way. Learn them now.

01

The SHSAT determines admission to NYC's specialized high schools on its own — no interview, no essay, no GPA. The score is the whole application.

02

The digital SAT adapts as you go: how you perform on the first module of a section decides how hard — and how valuable — the second module is.

03

There's no guessing penalty on the SAT anymore. An answer left blank and a wrong answer cost you the same — so always fill something in.

04

Twenty focused minutes a day beats one three-hour cram session most students try the week before. Consistency compounds; cramming just exhausts you.
