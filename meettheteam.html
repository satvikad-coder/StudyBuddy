Meet Us

Satvika Dudala

Co-Founder/Project Manager

Hi, I am Satvika Dudala, a Kode With Klossy Web Dev Scholar and a Studdy Buddy co-founder! Study Buddy came to me as an idea when my time as a rising junior was filled with struggle to find the right resources to study and prepare. After thoughtful consideration and hard work presented by the team, we bring you Study Buddy! I aspire to become an AI engineer in the future, so the skills I learnt during my time making this website taught me fundamentals of coding! I also learned how to manage, lead, organize, and collaborate with the wonderful Study Buddy team! I hope you find the resources quite useful, as the team and I worked hard to find the right resources! Thanks!

Dilnara Tairova

Co-Founder/Visonary

Hello! My name is Dilnara Tairova, and I'm one of the co-founders of Study Buddy. I am going to be a graduate of the class of 2030, and I personally took the SHSAT in November of 2025! I realized that the people who paid thousands of dollars for tutors did not really benefit from it. So, instead of paying that much for something that isn't really helpful, we decided to create something free and useful. We hope you like our website and find it resourceful for your exams.

Aaliyah Mansfield

Co-Founder/Coder

Hi! I am Aaliyah Mansfield. I like reading, drawing, and coding. I took the SHSAT in 2026. I was super nervous, and the whole time I was looking for good resources to study with.
