What is the SHSAT?

The Specialized High School Admissions Test (SHSAT) is an entrance exam used for admission into eight of New York City's specialized high schools. It tests students in English Language Arts and Math.

What is the SAT?

The SAT is a standardized test used by many colleges during the admissions process. It measures reading, writing, and math skills.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make test preparation less stressful by giving students one place to find reliable study material.

What We Want To Do

We want to make studying for the SHSAT and SAT easier, more organized, and less overwhelming.

Our website brings together helpful resources so students can spend less time searching and more time preparing.
